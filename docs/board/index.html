Polish rules what "finished" means for #459–#463

  • One font, five sizes. 34 title, 20 smart line, 16 rows, 14 second lines, 12 strip. Rows use two weights (500 name, 400 detail); titles take the Theme's weight.
  • Spacing in steps of 4 and 8. Side gutter 20 (was 22, so 360 px phones keep room), 32 between sections, rows at least 58 tall, taps at least 44.
  • Class names never wrap or break. The full name shows if it fits on one line at that phone's width; otherwise the short name (the student's own, then the timetable's, then initials). The board measures this live: switch to Small 360 and long names turn into short ones.
  • Task titles may end in "…" on one line; the full title is in the sheet. A task row never grows to 3 lines.
  • Numbers line up. Percentages and times sit in a fixed left column with tabular figures.
  • Late never piles up on Today. One line, "3 Late · oldest 104 days · Look through", however many there are.
  • The title fits on one line. "Good morning, Aishwarya." becomes "Morning, Aishwarya."; if that is still too wide, the title is "Today" and the greeting moves into the small line above it.
  • Big screens are filled with information, not wider rows. Laptop: two columns. PC: three. Lines stay under about 70 characters.
  • The Real data desk becomes a test fixture. Each build PR attaches its screenshots at 360, 393 and 412, and at 1280 and 1920, next to this board.

The 4 Themes same font and layout, Light and Dark

With one font, a Theme is its colours, its title weight and one signature "now" detail. Green, red and the Late icon never change. The accent only marks now; a free gap is always a hatched outline, so red or pink never means free.

Theme vs Light / Dark / Auto

Two separate settings in Settings → Look. The words match GLOSSARY.md.

Theme
Colours, title style and one "now" detail. Highlighter, Old Campus, Campus Sunset or Lo-fi. Old Campus is the default.
Light / Dark / Auto
Whether the screen is light or dark. Auto follows the phone. Every Theme works in both.

Today C2: the rework

C with the audit's fixes, working. The strip keeps names that fit and goes quiet when they don't; rows always carry the names. Tap a row for its sheet, swipe it to act. Switch the timetable to Busy or Split lab to stress it, and the device to Laptop.

    Rules this build follows

    • One card per screen at most: the dark "now" card. Everything else is rows and type.
    • Strip: spans your first class to your last. Each block tries, in order: the full name, the short form you typed (DBMS, COA; if you typed one, nothing else is made up), then one made from the name (Object Oriented Programming → OOP; Data Structures Lab → DS Lab, then DS; Maths III → M3). The first that fits whole is used, and two subjects never share a label. If none fits, the block stays plain and its row names it.
    • Rows: full names, wrapping to two lines instead of being cut.
    • Every action has a tap path: swipes are shortcuts, and the row's sheet has all the same choices.
    • Live app tools kept: quick add, Move, "+N more", the earlier-classes fold, Didn't happen, On duty, Undo, Study Shortcuts.
    • Laptop: two calm columns capped at 980px, floating sidebar, sheets open from the right.
